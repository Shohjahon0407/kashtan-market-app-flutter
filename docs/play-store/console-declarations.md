# Play Console deklaratsiyalari

Bu qiymatlar production funksiyalari bilan bir xil saqlanadi:

- App category: `Shopping`
- Countries/regions: `Uzbekistan` only
- Target audience: `13+`; ilova 13 yoshdan kichiklarga mo‘ljallanmagan
- Ads: `No`
- User-generated content: `Yes` — mahsulot sharhlari va video izohlari
- UGC himoyasi: qoidalarni qabul qilish, report, user block va admin moderation
- In-app purchases: `No` — to‘lovlar real jismoniy mahsulotlar uchun, Play
  Billing ishlatilmaydi
- Account creation: `Yes`, telefon OTP orqali
- Account deletion: ilova ichida va
  `https://api.beshkashtan.uz/delete-account/` orqali
- App access restricted: `Yes`; reviewer ma’lumoti `app-access.md`da
- Privacy policy: `https://api.beshkashtan.uz/privacy/`

Content rating savollarida shopping, location, online interaction va UGC
haqiqiy funksiyalarga mos ko‘rsatiladi; reklama, gambling yoki digital-goods
sotuvlari mavjud deb belgilanmaydi.
