# Xozmak category photography

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Each category uses a separate prompt and output, not a sliced contact sheet. Final assets: `assets/categories/household/<slug>.webp`, 768 × 768, WebP quality 83. PNG originals stay in the tool-managed generated-images directory.

## Shared brief

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square category image for a household shopping app, NOT a UI screenshot. Create a very natural professional product photograph, not illustration or CGI. Scene/backdrop: matte {palette} tabletop and matching clean seamless backdrop. Subject: {subject}. Composition: a small believable collection of objects centred inside the middle 70% of the square, all essential details safe inside a centred 4:3 crop; camera at a gentle three-quarter angle; generous breathing room. Lighting: large soft daylight from a window, realistic subtle contact shadows, neutral white balance. Materials: authentic textures, tiny natural imperfections, physically correct proportions and functional construction. No people, words, typography, labels, logos, watermark, fake 3D gloss, green colour cast or plants. One standalone square photograph only.
